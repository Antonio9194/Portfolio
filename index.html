About Station information

I have always loved languages, which first led me to work in the hospitality industry for 8 years, managing hotel operations and developing strong communication and problem-solving skills while interacting with people from around the world.

Later, I decided to explore a new kind of language — coding — and completed the full-stack web development course at Le Wagon. Since then I've built multiple projects, collaborated with other developers, and led the development of a hotel website as team lead.

Today I ship native iOS apps on top of Ruby on Rails backends — two of them live on the App Store — and bring real-world business experience to practical, user-focused software.

A01

Soul Bound Habit RPG · iOS

A habit tracker built as a role-playing game. Describe the life you're trying to build; it writes your daily quests, and your character levels up only as far as you actually do. Quests pay XP, coins and gems, and can drop a chest with gear you keep.

Native SwiftUI over a Rails JSON backend: Sign in with Apple, StoreKit 2 gem bundles verified server-side, Home and Lock Screen widgets, 3D chests in SceneKit, eleven languages. Live on the App Store since 14 September 2026.

  • Ruby on Rails
  • PostgreSQL
  • Swift · SwiftUI
  • StoreKit 2
  • OpenAI API

A02

Velora Expense tracker · iOS

Say "lunch, 1,200 yen" or photograph the receipt and the expense is logged — an AI assistant fills in title, amount, category and date. Monthly budget and income, daily limit, a calendar of spending, subscriptions, charts and savings jars.

Native iOS over Rails and PostgreSQL. Sign in with Apple and Google, push notifications, Home and Lock Screen widgets, a free plan with metered AI entries and a Plus subscription through StoreKit 2 with a one-week trial. Eight languages.
